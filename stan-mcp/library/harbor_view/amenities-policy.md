# Amenities Policy of Harbor View Condominium Association
<!-- type: amenities; adopted: 2020-03-04; amended: 2026-01-12 -->

Harbor View Condominiums offers several shared amenities included in the monthly assessment, along with reservable spaces that carry their own fees and deposits. This policy sets the hours, access rules, and booking procedures for the pool and gym, the rooftop terrace, the party room, the service elevator, guest parking, the package room, and the garage EV chargers, all managed through BuildingLink and the concierge desk.

## Article 1 - Pool and Gym

### 1.1 Hours and access

The 4th-floor pool and gym are open from 5:00 am to 10:00 pm daily and are accessed with an Owner's or registered Occupant's fob; the amenity is not reservable and operates on a first-come basis. An Owner may bring up to two guests per visit, and guests must be accompanied by the Owner or Occupant at all times while in the amenity.

### 1.2 Age and supervision rules

The gym is restricted to residents and guests age 16 and older. Children are welcome in the pool only when accompanied by an adult at all times; an unaccompanied child in the pool area is a violation handled under the Fine and Enforcement Policy's pool and clubhouse rule, which carries a $100.00 fine and the possibility of losing amenity access for up to 30 days for a serious or repeated violation.

## Article 2 - Rooftop Terrace

### 2.1 Hours and seasonal operation

The rooftop terrace is open from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm, seasonally from April through October, reflecting Bayport's weather and the terrace's open-air design. Outside this season the terrace is closed to general access, though the Rooftop and Gardens Committee may arrange maintenance access as needed to winterize planters and furniture. The terrace reopens each spring once the Committee confirms the planters, railings, and furniture have been inspected and any winter damage repaired.

### 2.2 Rules and grilling

No glass containers of any kind are permitted on the rooftop terrace, to prevent breakage on the open deck high above the street and waterfront below. Grilling on the terrace is available only through the attendant on duty for a scheduled event; a resident may not bring a personal grill to the terrace, consistent with the building-wide prohibition on personal grills on balconies. The attendant supplies and operates the grill equipment for a booked event so that residents are never managing open flame unsupervised on a shared rooftop surface.

### 2.3 Booking a private event

General terrace access during open hours is free and does not require a reservation; a private event on the terrace, such as a celebration reserving the space for a group, costs $150.00 and may be booked up to 60 days in advance through BuildingLink or with management. A private event booking includes the grill attendant's availability and any setup coordination with the Rooftop and Gardens Committee.

## Article 3 - Party Room

### 3.1 Hours and capacity

The party room is open from 9:00 am to 11:00 pm and accommodates up to 40 people. It is a reservable space suited to resident gatherings, celebrations, and committee or Board meetings held outside the regular meeting schedule, and is the usual location for the Board's monthly meetings as well as private resident functions. Furniture in the room may be rearranged for an event but must be returned to its standard layout before the reservation ends.

### 3.2 Fee, deposit, and booking

Reserving the party room costs $100.00 plus a $500.00 refundable deposit, charged to the reserving Owner's account. Bookings may be made up to 90 days in advance through BuildingLink; the deposit is returned after the event once management confirms no damage or excess cleaning was required. The reserving Owner is responsible for the conduct of all guests at the event and must leave the room by the end of the booked window so that a following reservation is not delayed.

## Article 4 - Service Elevator

### 4.1 Hours and purpose

The service elevator runs for moves and deliveries from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on weekdays and from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm on Saturdays, and does not operate for moves on Sundays. It must be booked for any move or delivery of furniture or large items, and protective pads are installed in the cab before a scheduled use so that walls and doors are not scratched. Using a passenger elevator for a move or large delivery instead of the booked service elevator is itself a rule violation, since it bypasses the protective pads and scheduling that keep hallways clear for other residents.

### 4.2 Fee, deposit, and booking lead time

Booking the service elevator requires a $150.00 refundable deposit and must be made at least five days in advance through BuildingLink or with management. The deposit is returned after the move or delivery, less any charge for damage the move caused to the service elevator, hallways, or Common Elements along the route. An Owner booking on behalf of a contractor or moving company remains responsible for the deposit and for ensuring the crew keeps to the scheduled window.

## Article 5 - Guest Parking

### 5.1 Availability and registration

The garage has eight visitor parking spaces available 24 hours a day, free of charge, for stays of up to 24 hours. A guest's vehicle must be registered with the concierge, either in advance or upon the guest's arrival, giving the vehicle's description, license plate, and the Unit it is visiting. An unregistered vehicle left in a visitor space may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense after the concierge attempts to identify and contact the driver.

### 5.2 Same-day booking

Guest parking is booked the same day through the concierge rather than through advance reservation in BuildingLink, reflecting its short-term, drop-in nature. An Owner expecting a guest staying longer than 24 hours should arrange alternative parking, since the eight visitor spaces are shared among all 180 Units and are not assigned to any one Unit. During a busy period, such as a holiday weekend, the concierge may ask an Owner to confirm how long a guest's vehicle will remain so the spaces can be managed fairly.

## Article 6 - Package Room

### 6.1 Access and pickup window

The package room is accessible 24 hours a day using an Owner's or Occupant's fob, and holds deliveries logged by the concierge through BuildingLink, which sends a notification when a package arrives for a Unit. Packages must be collected within 7 days of arrival; a package left beyond that window may be relocated by management to free up space for new deliveries, and management will attempt to contact the Owner before relocating anything.

### 6.2 Perishable items

A perishable delivery, such as groceries or a food order, is held in the package room's refrigerator for up to 48 hours rather than at room temperature, and the concierge notifies the Owner when a perishable item arrives so it can be collected promptly. An item not collected within the 48-hour refrigerated window is discarded, since the Association cannot guarantee food safety beyond that period.

## Article 7 - Garage EV Chargers

### 7.1 Availability and billing

The garage's 20 EV chargers are available to Owners 24 hours a day and are not reservable in advance; electricity used is billed at $0.28 per kilowatt-hour through the Owner's monthly account, separate from the regular $612.00 assessment. A charger's usage is metered individually so each Owner is billed only for the electricity their own vehicle uses, and the charge appears as a separate line item on the Owner's monthly BuildingLink statement.

### 7.2 Session limits when others are waiting

When another vehicle is waiting to use a charger, the vehicle currently charging is limited to a four-hour session so that the shared chargers remain available to all Owners with electric vehicles. An Owner whose vehicle is fully charged is expected to move it promptly when another resident is waiting, and repeated failure to do so when asked by the concierge may be treated as a parking violation under the Rules and Regulations.
