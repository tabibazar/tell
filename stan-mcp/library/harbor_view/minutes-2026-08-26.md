# Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting, August 26, 2026
<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-08-26 -->

The Board of Directors of Harbor View Condominium Association met on Wednesday, August 26, 2026, at 7:00 pm in the party room on the 4th floor and by video. Directors Richard Stone (President), Grace Liu (Treasurer), Ahmed Rahman (Secretary), Sofia Marino, and Daniel Price were present, constituting a quorum. Elena Varga, general manager for Coastal Residential, also attended. These minutes were posted in draft to BuildingLink and are subject to approval at the next regular meeting.

## Business

### 1. Call to order and approval of minutes

President Stone called the meeting to order at 7:02 pm and confirmed a quorum of all five directors. The Board reviewed the minutes of the July 2026 regular meeting, previously posted to BuildingLink in draft form for Owner review. Director Marino moved to approve the July minutes as posted; Director Price seconded; the motion carried 5-0, and the Secretary was directed to finalize them in the records library.

### 2. Treasurer's report

Treasurer Liu reported that the Association's operating accounts were on budget through July, with utilities running slightly under projection for the summer months. She noted that four Units remained more than 30 days past due on assessments, a number consistent with prior months, and that management was following the Collection Policy's notice schedule on those accounts. The Board accepted the Treasurer's report without objection.

### 3. Elevator modernization update

Ms. Varga reported that LiftCo had completed its inspection of the two passenger elevator cars, Elevator A and Elevator B, and had provided a preliminary estimate of $640,000.00 to modernize both cars, including new controllers, cab interiors, and door operators. She noted that the building's service elevator was not included in this scope. Treasurer Liu stated that funding this project from reserves was not advisable given the reserve fund's current 54 percent funding level identified in the 2025 reserve study, and that a special assessment was the more prudent approach. The Board directed management to request a firm, itemized proposal from LiftCo for the September meeting and to begin preparing a special assessment proposal for Board consideration.

### 4. Balcony inspection program

Ms. Varga reminded the Board that the building's balconies are due for their periodic structural inspection, last completed at the required five-year interval, and that a vendor would need to be engaged in the coming months to inspect all 180 Units' balconies for cracking, corrosion, and waterproofing condition. Director Price asked that the inspection be scheduled to minimize disruption to Owners, and management agreed to propose a floor-by-floor schedule at a future meeting.

### 5. Seasonal and maintenance notices

Ms. Varga reported that the rooftop terrace's seasonal hours, 8:00 am to 10:00 pm from April through October, would end for the year at the close of October, and that the Rooftop and Gardens Committee was planning planter maintenance before the terrace closes for the season. She also noted that the building's semiannual window cleaning, performed in April and October, was scheduled for the second week of October, and that a building-wide fire alarm test was being arranged for mid-October with advance notice to all Owners.

### 6. Homeowner forum

An owner of Unit 1202 asked whether the Board had considered adding more EV chargers given demand in the garage; management responded that a garage EV charger expansion was already being studied and would be placed on a future agenda. An owner of Unit 608 asked about the status of the community's reserve funding; Treasurer Liu summarized the 2025 reserve study's findings on the elevators and the garage membrane. No other Owners signed in to speak.

### 7. Adjournment

There being no further business, Director Rahman moved to adjourn; Director Marino seconded; the motion carried 5-0, and the meeting adjourned at 8:10 pm. The next regular meeting was set for September 23, 2026, at 7:00 pm in the party room and by video, with the elevator proposal and balcony inspection schedule to return as standing agenda items. Secretary Rahman confirmed the draft minutes of this meeting would be posted to BuildingLink within 30 days for Owner review ahead of that meeting.
