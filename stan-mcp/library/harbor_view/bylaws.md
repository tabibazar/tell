# Bylaws of Harbor View Condominium Association
<!-- type: bylaws; adopted: 2008-06-12; amended: 2020-03-04 -->

These Bylaws govern the administration of Harbor View Condominium Association, the nonprofit corporation that operates Harbor View Condominiums under the Condominium Declaration. They set out how the Board of Directors is elected and conducts business, how Owners vote and attend meetings, and how the Association keeps its records. They were adopted with the Declaration in 2008 and amended in 2020 to update meeting and voting procedures.

## Article 1 - Membership

### 1.1 Members

Every Owner of a Unit is automatically a member of the Association for as long as the Owner holds title to the Unit. Membership cannot be transferred except with the Unit itself, and a Unit with more than one Owner shares a single membership, voting as described in Article 4. A tenant renting a Unit under a registered lease is not a member of the Association and has no vote, since membership follows ownership, not occupancy.

### 1.2 Voting by unit share

Each Unit carries one vote, weighted by the Unit's ownership share as set out in the Declaration's schedule of interests, which generally reflects the Unit's size. A Unit's vote may be cast by any Owner of that Unit, or by a proxy the Owner has validly appointed under Section 4.2, but only one vote may be cast per Unit regardless of how many people hold title to it.

## Article 2 - Board of Directors

### 2.1 Composition

The Board consists of five directors elected by the membership. As of this document's most recent update, the Board is composed of Richard Stone (President, term ending 2027), Grace Liu (Treasurer, term ending 2027), Ahmed Rahman (Secretary, term ending 2026), Sofia Marino (Director, term ending 2026), and Daniel Price (Director, term ending 2028). Terms are staggered so that only some seats are up for election in any given year.

### 2.2 Terms and elections

Directors serve terms that expire at the annual meeting in the year shown for their seat; a director whose term "ends 2026" serves through the annual meeting held in that year and is then up for re-election or replacement. The next annual meeting is scheduled for March 2027, at which three seats will be on the ballot, reflecting the two seats ending in 2026 together with any vacancy or seat up for renewal at that meeting. Nominations are accepted from any Owner in good standing, and election is by the voting procedure in Article 4.

### 2.3 Powers and duties

The Board manages the business and affairs of the Association, including adopting the annual budget, levying assessments and special assessments, enforcing the Governing Documents, hiring and supervising the managing agent, awarding contracts such as those with LiftCo for elevator service and Sparkle Commercial for cleaning, and maintaining the reserve fund. The Board acts by majority vote of directors present at a properly noticed meeting at which a quorum is present.

### 2.4 Board meetings and quorum

The Board meets regularly, typically monthly, with notice posted through BuildingLink and in the lobby at least 72 hours in advance except in an emergency. A quorum of the Board is a majority of its five seats, meaning at least three directors. Any Owner may attend a Board meeting and speak during the open forum portion by signing in, with each speaker limited to two minutes.

### 2.5 Officers

The Board elects a President, Treasurer, and Secretary from among its directors at the first Board meeting following each annual meeting. The President presides at meetings and is the Association's chief officer; the Treasurer oversees the budget, financial statements, and the reserve fund; the Secretary keeps the minutes and the Association's official records. An officer serves until the next annual Board organizational meeting or until a successor is chosen.

### 2.6 Vacancies

A vacancy on the Board, whether from resignation, removal, or an uncompleted term, is filled by a majority vote of the remaining directors for the balance of the unexpired term, unless the Declaration or state law requires an election by the membership. A director filling a vacancy serves until the next annual meeting, at which the seat is included on the ballot for its originally scheduled term if applicable.

## Article 3 - Committees

### 3.1 Renovation Review Committee

The Renovation Review Committee reviews applications for interior renovations that touch plumbing, electrical systems, walls, or floors, confirms that a licensed and insured contractor is engaged, collects the required deposit, and verifies compliance with the acoustic flooring standard before issuing approval. The Committee reports its decisions to the Board and to management for recordkeeping in BuildingLink. Committee members serve staggered terms set by the Board and typically include at least one director to ensure the Committee's recommendations align with Board priorities.

### 3.2 Rooftop and Gardens Committee

The Rooftop and Gardens Committee looks after the planters and landscaping on the rooftop terrace, recommends seasonal plantings, and advises the Board on scheduling for private events on the terrace. Committee members are Owners appointed by the Board who serve at the Board's pleasure, and the Committee typically meets in the spring and fall to plan seasonal planting and closing of the terrace for the winter.

### 3.3 Committee appointment

The Board appoints committee members from among interested Owners, and may create additional committees as needed, such as a special committee to oversee a capital project like the elevator modernization. A committee has only the authority the Board delegates to it and reports its recommendations to the Board for final decision. An Owner interested in serving on a committee may express interest to management or at a Board meeting during the open forum.

## Article 4 - Meetings and Voting

### 4.1 Annual meeting

The annual meeting of the membership is held each year, with the next annual meeting scheduled for March 2027, at which three Board seats will be elected and the prior year's business is reported to Owners. Notice of the annual meeting, including the agenda and any proxy form, is distributed at least 14 days in advance through BuildingLink and posted in the lobby.

### 4.2 Proxies

An Owner unable to attend a meeting in person may appoint another person, including another Owner or a household member, as proxy to cast that Unit's vote, by a signed proxy form delivered to management before the meeting begins. A proxy is valid only for the meeting specified and may be revoked by the Owner at any time before it is exercised, including by the Owner's own appearance at the meeting.

### 4.3 Quorum for membership meetings

A quorum for any meeting of the membership, including the annual meeting and any special meeting, is 33 percent of the total unit ownership shares, present in person or by proxy. If a quorum is not reached, the meeting may be adjourned and reconvened at a later date with the same notice requirements, at which a reduced quorum may apply if the Declaration permits.

### 4.4 Special meetings

A special meeting of the membership may be called by the President, by a majority of the Board, or by a written petition signed by Owners holding at least 20 percent of the total ownership shares, to address business that cannot wait until the next annual meeting, such as approving a special assessment. Notice must state the purpose of the special meeting, and only that business may be conducted.

### 4.5 Speaking at meetings

An Owner wishing to address the Board or the membership at a meeting, including during the open forum at a regular Board meeting, signs in before the meeting starts and is allotted two minutes to speak. The Board may extend or limit this time at its discretion to keep the meeting orderly, and written comments may be submitted in advance through management for the record.

## Article 5 - Records and Communications

### 5.1 Official records

The Association's official records, including the minutes of Board and membership meetings, financial statements, the reserve study, contracts, and the Governing Documents, are maintained in the BuildingLink library, where Owners may access them at any reasonable time. Draft minutes are posted within 30 days of a meeting and finalized at the next meeting of the same body. An Owner who cannot locate a document in BuildingLink may request a copy from management during office hours.

### 5.2 Financial records

The Treasurer, with the managing agent, maintains the Association's financial books, prepares a monthly financial report for the Board, and is responsible for the annual budget process described in the 2026 Budget and Reserve Study. An Owner may request to inspect the Association's financial records on reasonable notice to management, and the Treasurer presents a summary of the Association's financial position at each annual meeting.

### 5.3 Notices to owners

Routine notices to Owners, including meeting notices, assessment reminders, and maintenance notices such as elevator outages or water shutoffs, are distributed through BuildingLink, posted in the lobby, and relayed by the concierge where urgent. An Owner is responsible for keeping a current email address on file with management to receive these notices, and management is not responsible for a missed notice sent to an outdated address.

## Article 6 - Amendment of Bylaws

### 6.1 Amendment procedure

These Bylaws may be amended by the vote required for amending the Declaration under Article 9 of that document, at a membership meeting called for that purpose with notice describing the proposed amendment, or by written ballot where the Declaration and state law permit. An amendment to these Bylaws takes effect when adopted and recorded as required by the Declaration.
