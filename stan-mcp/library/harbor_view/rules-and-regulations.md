# Rules and Regulations of Harbor View Condominium Association
<!-- type: rules; adopted: 2026-01-12 -->

These Rules and Regulations implement the Condominium Declaration and Bylaws of Harbor View Condominium Association and govern everyday life in the building for all Owners, tenants, and guests. The Board updated them most recently in January 2026. They are enforced under the Fine and Enforcement Policy and do not replace the Declaration, which controls if there is any conflict.

## Article 1 - Noise and Conduct

### 1.1 Quiet hours

Quiet hours in Harbor View run from 10:00 pm to 8:00 am every day of the week. During quiet hours, Owners and Occupants must keep music, television, conversation, and foot traffic at a level that cannot be heard in a neighboring Unit or the hallway. Construction and renovation noise is never permitted outside the weekday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm renovation window described in Article 5, regardless of quiet hours.

### 1.2 Smoking

Smoking of any kind, including cigarettes, cigars, vaping devices, and cannabis, is prohibited everywhere in the building, including inside individual Units and on balconies. This rule exists because the tower's ventilation and air-handling systems carry smoke between Units, and it is enforced the same as the Declaration's other use restrictions, including the fine schedule in the Fine and Enforcement Policy.

### 1.3 Drones

Drones may not be flown over another Unit's balcony, over the pool, or over the rooftop terrace while people are using either amenity. An Owner who wants to fly a drone from a private balcony over open water or away from occupied areas should check with the concierge first to confirm it will not disturb another resident, and should never fly over a hallway, the lobby, or any indoor Common Element.

## Article 2 - Pets

### 2.1 Number and size

A Unit may keep at most two pets. Any dog kept in the building must weigh less than 50 pounds; there is no weight limit for cats or other small household pets, but all pets of any kind must be registered with management. A new pet brought into a Unit, whether permanent or a temporary pet-sitting arrangement longer than a few days, should be registered before it moves in.

### 2.2 Registration and leashing

Every pet must be registered with management through BuildingLink, including a description and, for a dog, its approximate weight. Pets must be leashed at all times in any Common Element, including the lobby, hallways, and garage; an unleashed pet in a Common Element is a violation even if the pet is well behaved and under the Owner's voice control, and the leash requirement applies even for a short trip to the mailbox or trash chute.

### 2.3 Elevator use for pets

Owners and Occupants should use the service elevator rather than the passenger elevators when transporting pets, whenever the service elevator is free, to minimize shedding, dander, and odor in the finished passenger elevator cabs used by all residents. This is a courtesy expectation backed by the same enforcement process as other rules if it becomes a repeated problem, and does not apply when the service elevator is already in use for a scheduled move or delivery.

## Article 3 - Parking and Vehicles

### 3.1 Assigned and guest parking

Each Unit with a garage space parks only in its assigned space; spaces are Limited Common Elements appurtenant to specific Units and may not be used by another Owner without permission. The garage has eight visitor spaces available 24 hours a day for up to 24 hours at a time; a guest's vehicle must be registered with the concierge before or upon arrival.

### 3.2 Commercial vehicles

No commercial vehicle weighing over one ton may be parked overnight in the garage or in guest parking. A contractor's vehicle used for a registered renovation or delivery may park temporarily during the weekday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm work window described in Article 5, but must be removed from the garage by the end of the work day, and a contractor needing overnight parking must make separate arrangements off-site.

## Article 4 - Balconies, Trash, and Common Areas

### 4.1 Balcony use

Balconies may hold only plants and outdoor furniture. Grills of any kind, laundry lines or drying racks, storage boxes, and anything attached to or hung over the railing are prohibited on every balcony in the building. A grill found on a balcony is treated as a fire-safety matter and is subject to the enforcement procedure described in the Fine and Enforcement Policy, including the fine applicable to that violation.

### 4.2 Trash and recycling

Trash chutes are located on every floor and may be used at any time; recycling bins are provided in each chute room for sorted recyclables. Large or bulky items that do not fit the chute, such as furniture or appliance boxes, must be brought to the loading dock on Fridays rather than left in a hallway or chute room, and an Owner who needs disposal outside the Friday schedule should contact management to arrange it.

### 4.3 Satellite dishes

A satellite dish under 1 meter in diameter may be mounted on a Unit's balcony where needed for reception, and should face away from the street-facing side of the building where possible. A dish that cannot achieve reception in the preferred location, or that an Owner wishes to mount elsewhere, requires architectural approval under the Architectural and Renovation Guidelines before installation.

### 4.4 Holiday decorations

Holiday decorations may go up no more than 30 days before the holiday they celebrate and must come down within 15 days after it. Decorations on a balcony or in a window are permitted within this window; decorations may not be affixed to hallway doors, walls, or any other Common Element in a way that damages the surface or blocks egress.

## Article 5 - Renovations and Moving

### 5.1 Renovation work hours

Any renovation or repair work inside a Unit, including work that does not require a full renovation application, must be performed only on weekdays between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm, to limit noise and disruption for neighboring Units. Work touching plumbing, electrical systems, walls, or floors additionally requires the renovation application and deposit described in Article 4 of the Condominium Declaration.

### 5.2 Moves and deliveries

All moves in or out of a Unit, and any delivery of furniture or large items, must use the service elevator, which must be booked in advance with management at least five days ahead and requires a $150 refundable deposit. Moves may take place on weekdays from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm or on Saturdays from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm; the service elevator does not run for moves on Sundays. Management installs protective pads in the service elevator before a scheduled move.

### 5.3 Flooring standard

Any hard-surface flooring, such as tile, hardwood, or laminate, installed in a Unit must sit on an acoustic underlayment rated Impact Insulation Class 50 or better, so that impact noise is not transmitted to the Unit below. This standard applies whether or not the flooring work is part of a larger renovation requiring the Article 4 application, and applies equally to a small repair, such as replacing a section of damaged flooring.

## Article 6 - Short-Term Rentals and Leasing

### 6.1 Prohibition on short-term rentals

Short-term and transient rentals of any length are not permitted in any Unit at Harbor View. The minimum lease term for any Unit is 12 months, and every lease, regardless of length, must be registered with management before the tenant takes occupancy. A violation of this rule is fined at $500.00 per stay with no cure period, since the unauthorized stay cannot be reversed once it has occurred.

### 6.2 Registering a tenant

An Owner who leases a Unit must give management the tenant's name and contact information and a copy of the signed lease or an approved summary before the tenant moves in, using the move-in procedure in Section 5.2. The Owner remains responsible for the tenant's and any subtenant's compliance with these Rules, including any fine the tenant's conduct causes to be assessed against the Unit.

## Article 7 - Signs and Flags

### 7.1 Signs

An Owner may display one sign in the Unit's own window of a size customary for residential display, such as a notice for a private sale of personal property; no sign may be placed in a hallway, on a balcony railing, in the lobby, or on any other Common Element without the Board's prior permission. Real estate "for sale" signage for a Unit is handled through the concierge and lobby directory rather than exterior signage, since the building has no yards or street frontage per Unit.

## Article 8 - Garage and EV Chargers

### 8.1 EV charger sessions

The garage's 20 EV chargers are available 24 hours a day and bill electricity at $0.28 per kilowatt-hour through the monthly account. When another vehicle is waiting to charge, a charging session is limited to four hours so that the shared chargers remain available to all Owners with electric vehicles, and the concierge can be asked to post a courtesy reminder on a vehicle that has finished charging.

### 8.2 Home EV equipment

A Unit's garage parking space may not host a separate home charging unit without architectural approval, since garage electrical capacity and conduit runs are shared Common Elements; an Owner wanting dedicated charging beyond the shared EV chargers should submit a request to the Renovation Review Committee for an engineering review of available capacity before any equipment is purchased or installed in the assigned space.

## Article 9 - Enforcement Reference

### 9.1 How these rules are enforced

A violation of these Rules and Regulations is handled under the Fine and Enforcement Policy, beginning with a courtesy notice for most first-time issues and escalating through further notices, fines, and hearings for repeated or serious violations, including the $100 fine and possible 30-day loss of amenity access that applies when a pool or clubhouse rule is broken. Owners may appeal a notice through the hearing process described in that policy.
