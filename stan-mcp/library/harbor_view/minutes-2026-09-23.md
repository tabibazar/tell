# Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting, September 23, 2026
<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-09-23 -->

The Board of Directors of Harbor View Condominium Association met on Wednesday, September 23, 2026, at 7:00 pm in the party room on the 4th floor and by video. Directors Richard Stone (President), Grace Liu (Treasurer), Ahmed Rahman (Secretary), Sofia Marino, and Daniel Price were present, constituting a quorum. Elena Varga, general manager for Coastal Residential, also attended. These minutes were posted in draft to BuildingLink and are subject to approval at the next regular meeting.

## Business

### 1. Call to order and approval of minutes

President Stone called the meeting to order at 7:00 pm and confirmed a quorum of all five directors. Director Price moved to approve the minutes of the August 26, 2026 meeting as posted; Director Liu seconded; the motion carried 5-0, and the Secretary noted that the finalized minutes would be posted to the BuildingLink records library within 30 days, consistent with the Bylaws' recordkeeping requirement.

### 2. Treasurer's report

Treasurer Liu reported that the Association remained on budget for the 2026 fiscal year through August, with the operating and reserve accounts both in line with the adopted budget. She noted that the reserve fund balance stood at $1,180,000.00, consistent with the 2025 reserve study's finding of approximately 54 percent funding, and that the $230,000.00 annual reserve contribution budgeted for 2026 was being deposited on schedule. The Board accepted the report without objection.

### 3. Elevator modernization special assessment

Ms. Varga presented LiftCo's firm proposal of $640,000.00 to modernize Elevator A and Elevator B, unchanged from the preliminary estimate reviewed in August. After discussion, Director Liu moved that management prepare a special assessment proposal of approximately $3,550.00 per Unit, payable over 12 months, for presentation and a vote at the October 28, 2026 meeting, together with notice to all Owners at least 14 days in advance as the Bylaws require. Director Stone seconded; the motion carried 5-0. The Board directed management to post the full proposal, including the monthly installment schedule, to BuildingLink ahead of the October meeting.

### 4. Balcony inspection program

Ms. Varga reported that a structural engineering vendor had been selected to perform the five-year balcony inspection required across all 180 Units, with inspections proposed to begin in November 2026 on a floor-by-floor schedule. Director Marino asked that Owners receive at least 14 days' notice before their floor's inspection date; management agreed this would be included in the inspection notice.

### 5. Renovation Review Committee report

The Renovation Review Committee reported one renovation application under review: a kitchen remodel with new hardwood flooring for Unit 1704, including the required acoustic underlayment specification. The Committee expected to complete its review and issue a decision by September 30, 2026, within the 30-day window required by the Declaration. No other renovation applications were pending, and the Committee noted the contractor's license and insurance certificate had already been submitted and verified.

### 6. Garage EV charger expansion

Director Price raised the EV charger demand question carried over from the August meeting. Ms. Varga reported that management was evaluating garage electrical capacity for additional chargers beyond the current 20 units and would bring a cost estimate to the October meeting alongside the elevator and balcony items already on that agenda. Director Marino asked that the estimate include whether existing garage wiring could support the expansion without a separate electrical upgrade project.

### 7. Homeowner forum

An owner of Unit 2104 asked whether the proposed special assessment could be paid in a lump sum rather than over 12 months; Treasurer Liu responded that the Board intended to allow a lump-sum option alongside the 12-month installment plan, to be confirmed when the proposal is finalized for the October vote. An owner of Unit 311 asked about the scope of the October balcony inspections; management confirmed the inspection covers structural condition only and is a Common Element expense under the Declaration. No other Owners signed in to speak.

### 8. Adjournment

There being no further business, Director Rahman moved to adjourn; Director Price seconded; the motion carried 5-0, and the meeting adjourned at 8:22 pm. The next regular meeting was set for Wednesday, October 28, 2026, at 7:00 pm in the party room and by video, with the elevator special assessment vote, balcony inspections, and the garage EV charger expansion estimate on the agenda.
