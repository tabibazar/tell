# Minutes of the Oak Ridge Estates Board Meeting - September 12, 2026
<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-09-12 -->

The Board of Directors of the Oak Ridge Estates Property Owners Association met Saturday, September 12, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. at the community barn. These minutes were approved at the Board's November 14, 2026 meeting.

## Business

### 1. Call to Order and Roll Call

President Walt Harmon called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. Treasurer Debbie Sloane and Roads Committee chair Frank Ito were present, constituting a full Board and a quorum under the Bylaws. Administrator Carol Benson attended to take notes. About twenty Owners attended, a larger turnout than the July meeting, which President Harmon attributed to interest in the Ridge Road paving project and the upcoming fall trail ride.

### 2. Approval of Minutes

Roads chair Ito moved to approve the minutes of the July 11, 2026 meeting as posted to the Association's website, with one correction noted by Treasurer Sloane to the spelling of a vendor's name in the roads report. Treasurer Sloane seconded the motion as corrected, and it carried 3-0. The corrected minutes were reposted to the website the following week.

### 3. Treasurer's Report

Treasurer Debbie Sloane reported the reserve fund balance at $211,000.00 as of the end of August, reflecting a quarter of interest earnings and no major capital withdrawals since the last report. She reported that assessment collections for the year remained strong, with only one account, Lot 31, carrying a past-due quarterly installment; she noted the fourth-quarter payment for that account was due November 1 under its approved payment plan. Ms. Sloane also previewed that the 2027 budget process would begin at the Board's October planning session, ahead of the figures being presented at the April 2027 annual meeting.

### 4. Roads Committee Report

Roads chair Frank Ito reported that Turner Excavating had returned a firm bid of $85,000.00 to grade and pave the lower half of Ridge Road, matching the preliminary estimate reported in July, and recommended the Board bring the contract to a vote at the November 14 meeting. Mr. Ito also announced that Turner Excavating would grade Ridge Road in preparation for paving on October 20 and 21, with drivers able to expect about a 10-minute wait during the work. Separately, Mr. Ito reported he was finalizing renewal terms with Hollis Plowing for the coming winter's snow contract, to be presented for a vote in November alongside the paving contract.

### 5. Trails and Barn Committee Report

The Trails and Barn Committee reported that the erosion repair near the lake identified in July had been completed ahead of the fall trail ride. The committee confirmed the lake dam's five-year inspection was scheduled for October and said the written report would be presented at the November 14 meeting. The committee also reported that planning for the October 25 fall trail ride and chili cook-off was on track, with the barn booked and several Owners already volunteered to help with the cook-off.

### 6. Architectural Committee Report

The administrator reported that the Architectural Review Committee had approved request ARC-2026-007, the Lot 14 owner's 40-by-60-foot horse barn, on August 3, 2026. The Board noted the approval for the record and confirmed the Owner's county building permit application remained pending with Hollis County; construction may not begin until that permit is issued. No other architectural requests were pending, and President Harmon thanked the committee for turning the review around promptly given the size of the project.

### 7. Enforcement Matters

The administrator reported opening violation OR-2026-012 against the owner of Lot 31 on September 12, 2026, for failing to provide a septic pumping receipt on file since 2021, as required under the wells and septic rule. The Board directed the administrator to send the Owner a written notice requiring a current pumping receipt by December 1, 2026, consistent with the Fine and Enforcement Policy's treatment of a documentary violation of this kind, which is resolved by providing the missing record rather than through a hearing. No fine was assessed at this stage, since this is a first notice on this issue.

### 8. New Business

The Board reviewed and finalized the agenda for its November 14, 2026 meeting: a vote on the Ridge Road paving contract with Turner Excavating for $85,000.00, a vote on renewing the Hollis Plowing snow contract, the lake dam inspection report, and a potluck lunch to follow. The Board also approved opening the annual driveway snow plowing sign-up program, at $35.00 per storm, with a sign-up deadline of November 15, 2026, and directed the administrator to notify all Owners by mail and email.

### 9. Homeowner Forum

During the homeowner forum, the owner of Lot 6 asked whether the Ridge Road paving project would affect access during the October 25 trail ride; Roads chair Ito confirmed the grading work would be finished by October 21, well before the event. The owner of Lot 40 asked about adding a fifth horse paddock given the waiting list, and the Trails and Barn Committee agreed to look into available land near the existing paddocks and report back. No formal action was taken on either comment.

### 10. Adjournment

With no further business, President Harmon adjourned the meeting at 11:35 a.m. The Board reminded Owners that the Ridge Road grading would take place October 20 and 21, that the fall trail ride and chili cook-off would follow on October 25, and that the Board's next regular meeting would be held Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. at the community barn, followed by a potluck lunch.
