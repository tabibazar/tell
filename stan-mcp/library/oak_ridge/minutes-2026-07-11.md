# Minutes of the Oak Ridge Estates Board Meeting - July 11, 2026
<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-07-11 -->

The Board of Directors of the Oak Ridge Estates Property Owners Association met Saturday, July 11, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. at the community barn. These minutes were approved, as corrected, at the Board's September 12, 2026 meeting.

## Business

### 1. Call to Order and Roll Call

President Walt Harmon called the meeting to order at 10:04 a.m. Treasurer Debbie Sloane and Roads Committee chair Frank Ito were also present, constituting a full Board and a quorum under the Bylaws. Administrator Carol Benson attended to take notes. Approximately fifteen Owners attended in the audience, representing roughly a dozen Lots, including the owners of Lot 14 and Lot 31. President Harmon welcomed those present and reminded everyone that the homeowner forum would follow the committee reports later in the meeting.

### 2. Approval of Minutes

The Board reviewed the minutes of its May 2026 meeting, previously posted to the Association's website for Owner review. Treasurer Sloane moved to approve the minutes as posted; Roads chair Ito seconded. The motion carried 3-0. President Harmon reminded Owners that approved minutes are posted to the Association's website within about a week of each meeting, and that any Owner may request a paper copy from the administrator during posted office hours.

### 3. Treasurer's Report

Treasurer Debbie Sloane reported that the Association's operating accounts were tracking close to the 2026 budget through June, with road maintenance spending slightly under budget due to a mild spring. She reported the reserve fund balance at approximately $208,000.00, before the most recent quarter's interest was posted, and noted that most Owners had paid their annual assessment in full by the January 31 due date or were current on an approved quarterly payment plan. Ms. Sloane flagged one account, for Lot 31, as past due on a quarterly installment and said the administrator was following up directly with the Owner before any late fee or further notice was issued.

### 4. Roads Committee Report

Roads chair Frank Ito reported that the committee had solicited bids from Turner Excavating for paving the lower half of Ridge Road, the next section due under the Association's 10-year paving plan, with a preliminary estimate of $85,000.00. Mr. Ito said the committee hoped to bring a firm bid and a recommendation to the Board's next meeting. He also reported that the Hollis Plowing snow contract for the coming winter would need to be renewed or rebid before the fall, and that he would bring renewal terms to the September meeting.

### 5. Trails and Barn Committee Report

The Trails and Barn Committee reported that the six miles of riding trails were in good condition after spring maintenance, with one section near the lake needing erosion repair before the fall trail ride. The committee confirmed the fishing lake's five-year dam inspection was due later in 2026 and said it would schedule the inspection and report findings to the Board once complete. The committee also reported the community barn's booking calendar was filling up for the fall, including the planned October 25 trail ride and chili cook-off.

### 6. Architectural Committee Report

The administrator reported that one architectural request was pending with the committee: ARC-2026-007, a request from the owner of Lot 14 to build a 40-by-60-foot horse barn. The committee was still reviewing the site plan and said it expected to issue a decision within the Guidelines' 30-day review period. No other architectural requests were pending as of the meeting, and the administrator noted that an Owner is free to call the barn office during posted hours to check on the status of a pending request rather than waiting for the next meeting's report.

### 7. Old Business

There was no old business carried over from the Board's May meeting requiring further discussion or a vote at this meeting. President Harmon noted that the Board's standing items, including the Ridge Road paving bid and the snow plow contract renewal, would be tracked as new business and committee reports at upcoming meetings rather than listed separately as old business until a specific decision point was reached.

### 8. New Business

President Harmon raised the Association's optional driveway snow plowing sign-up program for the coming winter, noting that the sign-up period would need to be announced to Owners well before the first snowfall. The Board agreed the administrator should prepare a notice for the fall, with a sign-up deadline in mid-November, at a cost of $35.00 per storm for any Owner who opts in. No vote was required, as this is a continuation of an existing program.

### 9. Homeowner Forum

During the homeowner forum, the owner of Lot 22 asked about the status of the horse paddock waiting list and was told by the administrator that two names remained ahead on the list. The owner of Lot 9 raised concerns about dust from unpaved sections of the road during the dry summer months, and Roads chair Ito said the committee would consider a calcium chloride treatment on the worst sections before the Ridge Road paving project began. No formal action was taken on either comment.

### 10. Adjournment

With no further business, President Harmon adjourned the meeting at 11:20 a.m. The Board thanked Owners for attending and reminded everyone that the Trails and Barn Committee would welcome volunteers ahead of the October 25 trail ride and chili cook-off. The Board's next regular meeting was set for September 12, 2026 at the community barn, with minutes from both meetings to be posted to the Association's website once approved.
