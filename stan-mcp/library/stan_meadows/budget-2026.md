# 2026 Budget and Reserve Study Summary for Stan Meadows Townhome Association

<!-- type: budget; adopted: 2025-12-09 -->

The Board adopted this budget in December 2025 for the 2026 fiscal year, which runs the calendar year. It sets the regular monthly assessment at $340 per Lot and summarizes the reserve study the Association commissioned in 2025. Figures are presented to the nearest dollar.

## Article 1 - Overview

### 1.1 Total operating budget

The 2026 operating budget is $391,680, funded entirely by the regular assessment: 96 Lots at $340 per month for 12 months equals $391,680. The budget carries no planned special assessment for 2026; any new capital need beyond what the reserve fund covers, such as the gate camera upgrade on the November 2026 agenda, is funded from reserves rather than an added assessment, as reflected in Article 3.

### 1.2 Minor and variable income

Guest suite fees ($60 a night plus a $50 cleaning fee per stay) and late fees collected under the Collection Policy are recorded as they are received and used to offset operating costs during the year, but are not budgeted as a fixed line because their amount varies. They do not change the $391,680 assessment-funded total above.

### 2.2 Roof and siding reserve

The largest single line, $140,000, is the annual contribution to the roof-and-siding capital reserve, reflecting that the Association, not individual Owners, owns the responsibility for roofs and exterior siding across all 16 buildings. This contribution is set using the 2025 reserve study described in Article 4 and is expected to rise gradually as the building stock ages.

### 2.3 Landscaping and common-area utilities

Landscaping, performed under contract by Evergreen Grounds on a weekly Wednesday schedule, is budgeted at $62,000 and covers front-yard care across all 96 Lots as well as common-area beds, the dog park perimeter, and the street-tree strips. Common-area utilities, budgeted at $14,000, cover electricity and water for the pool, fitness room, gate, and street lighting.

### 2.4 Gate, security and management

Gate and security costs, budgeted at $31,000, cover the SecureEntry Systems service contract, camera maintenance, and fob and app credential administration, separate from the one-time $18,000 capital upgrade funded from reserves described in Article 3. Management fees of $14,400 cover the Association's contract with Summit HOA Services for day-to-day administration, billing, and vendor coordination.

## Article 3 - Capital Projects Funded from Reserves

### 3.1 Gate camera upgrade

The Board is considering an $18,000 gate camera upgrade, funded from the reserve fund rather than the operating budget or a special assessment, on the agenda for the November 10, 2026 Board meeting. Funding it from reserves keeps the regular assessment unchanged for 2026 and 2027. The quote, obtained from SecureEntry Systems and presented to the Board in September 2026, covers new cameras and a recorder at the main gate to replace equipment nearing the end of its service life.

### 3.2 Building 7 siding repair

Siding repair on Building 7, including scaffolding scheduled for October 19 through 30, 2026, is funded from the roof and siding reserve described in Section 2.2 rather than as a separate line, since it falls within the purpose that reserve exists to cover. The Board approved the repair proposal from Peak Roofing at its September 2026 meeting after an inspection identified several warped panels, and management is notifying Building 7 residents of the scaffolding dates in advance.

## Article 4 - Reserve Study Summary

### 4.1 Current reserve position

The Association's capital reserve fund stands at $604,000 as of the 2025 reserve study, which calculates the fund as approximately 78% funded against a fully funded target of roughly $775,000. A reserve below 100% funded is common among associations of Stan Meadows' age and is not, by itself, a sign of financial distress, but the Board monitors the percentage each year when setting the reserve contribution in Article 2.2.

### 4.2 Major future expenditure

The 2025 reserve study identifies roof replacement for Buildings 1 through 8 as due in 2031, the largest single future capital item the reserve fund must cover. Buildings 9 through 16, completed in the same original construction phase, are projected to need roof replacement on a following cycle after 2031. The Board intends to review the reserve contribution rate at each annual budget cycle to stay on pace for the 2031 project without a special assessment.

### 4.3 Reserve study methodology

The reserve study inventories every major common-area component the Association is responsible for, including roofs, siding, the gate and access system, pool equipment, and fitness room equipment, estimates each component's remaining useful life and replacement cost, and recommends an annual funding level. The Board commissions an update to the reserve study at least every five years, consistent with industry practice, with the next full update expected in 2030 ahead of the 2031 roof project.
