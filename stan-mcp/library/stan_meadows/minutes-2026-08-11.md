# Minutes of the Board of Directors, Stan Meadows Townhome Association - August 11, 2026

<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-08-11 -->

These are the minutes of the regular monthly meeting of the Board of Directors of Stan Meadows Townhome Association, held Tuesday, August 11, 2026, at 6:30 pm in the fitness room lounge and by video, with management from Summit HOA Services in attendance.

## Business

### 1. Call to order and roll call

President Helen Cho called the meeting to order at 6:32 pm in the fitness room lounge, with the video line also open for Owners joining remotely. Also present were Treasurer Marcus Bell and Secretary Ivy Nguyen, constituting a full Board quorum, along with property manager Brian Kowalski of Summit HOA Services. Four Owners attended by video and two in person. Helen Cho reviewed the agenda circulated in advance in the resident app and noted no changes.

### 2. Approval of July minutes

Ivy Nguyen moved to approve the minutes of the July 14, 2026 regular meeting as circulated in the resident app. Marcus Bell seconded. The motion carried 3-0. No corrections were requested by the Board or by any Owner present, and the Secretary noted the approved minutes would be posted to the resident app within the week, consistent with the Association's usual practice.

### 3. Manager's report

Brian Kowalski reported routine operations for July: landscaping continued on its normal Wednesday schedule with Evergreen Grounds, the pool remained open with no incidents, and gate access logs showed normal visitor-pass usage. He noted that SecureEntry Systems had flagged the main gate camera as near end of life and recommended the Board consider a replacement before the next budget cycle, which led into the discussion under Item 6.

### 4. Treasurer's report

Marcus Bell reported that the Association remained on budget through July, with year-to-date operating expenses tracking slightly under the $391,680 annual budget, mainly due to a mild summer reducing common-area irrigation costs. He reported the reserve fund balance at approximately $598,000, consistent with the funding schedule set by the 2025 reserve study, and noted no new delinquencies requiring referral to the collections attorney this month.

### 5. Architectural Review Committee report

The Committee reported four requests reviewed at its August meeting: two door-colour changes approved within the six-option palette, one shed request approved pending a site visit to confirm the trim colour matches the Owner's building scheme, and one request for an outdoor EV charger approved for a side-yard location. No requests were denied in July.

### 6. Gate camera upgrade discussion

Following the manager's report under Item 3, the Board discussed replacing the main gate camera system. Brian Kowalski was asked to obtain a formal quote from SecureEntry Systems for an upgraded camera and recorder, to be funded from the reserve fund rather than a special assessment, for presentation at the September meeting. No motion was made; the item was tabled pending the quote.

### 7. Building 7 siding repair update

Marcus Bell reported that Peak Roofing had completed its inspection of Building 7's siding and identified several panels needing replacement due to age-related warping, consistent with the building stock's 2011 construction date. Management was asked to obtain a firm proposal and schedule, funded from the roof and siding reserve, for the Board's review in September.

### 8. Dog park turf proposal

A resident of Unit 9C spoke during the forum portion of a prior meeting about worn turf in the dog park; the Board asked management to obtain a replacement quote from a landscaping vendor for review once received, noting that any such project would also be funded from reserves rather than the operating budget.

### 9. Homeowner forum

A homeowner from Unit 6A asked whether the Association could add more visitor parking signage near Building 3, citing confusion among new residents' guests. Helen Cho asked management to review signage at that location. A homeowner from Unit 11D asked about the status of the gate camera discussed under Item 6 and was referred to that agenda item.

### 10. Adjournment

There being no further business, Helen Cho adjourned the meeting at 7:21 pm. The next regular meeting was set for Tuesday, September 8, 2026, at the same time and location, with the video option to remain available. Brian Kowalski agreed to circulate the gate camera quote from SecureEntry Systems to the Board by email as soon as it was received, so directors could review it ahead of the September discussion rather than seeing it for the first time at the meeting.
