# Amenities and Guest Suite Policy of Stan Meadows Townhome Association

<!-- type: amenities; adopted: 2012-02-01; amended: 2019-04-15, 2026-07-01 -->

Stan Meadows offers four shared amenities to residents: the pool, the fitness room, the dog park, and the guest suite. This policy sets the hours, access method, and rules for each, and the booking process for the guest suite, implementing the Owners' easement of enjoyment described in the Declaration.

## Article 1 - Pool

### 1.1 Season and hours

The pool is open from May 15 to September 30 each year, from 9 am to 10 pm daily. Outside this season, including from October 1 through mid-May, the pool is closed and the gate fob reader at the pool deck is deactivated. There is no lifeguard on duty at any time; residents and guests swim at their own risk.

### 1.2 Access and guests

Access is by gate fob. A household may bring up to four guests to the pool at a time; a guest count above four, or a guest left unaccompanied by a resident, is a violation under the Fine and Enforcement Policy. The pool area is not reservable for private use; it remains open to all residents during posted hours.

### 1.3 Pool conduct

Smoking is prohibited anywhere in the pool area, consistent with the Rules and Regulations. Glass containers are not permitted on the pool deck, given the injury risk around bare feet. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult resident or guardian at all times, and since there is no lifeguard, that supervision is the only safety oversight the pool area has.

## Article 2 - Fitness Room

### 2.1 Hours and access

The fitness room is open year-round from 5 am to 11 pm, accessed by gate fob. It is not reservable; equipment is used on a first-come basis, and a resident should be considerate of others waiting during the busier early-morning and evening hours by not monopolizing a single machine for an extended warm-up or rest period.

### 2.2 Age and equipment care

Use of the fitness room is limited to residents and guests age 16 and older, unaccompanied by a younger child, given the equipment involved. Users must wipe down equipment after use. Equipment malfunction or damage should be reported to management promptly so Summit HOA Services can arrange repair.

## Article 3 - Dog Park

### 3.1 Hours and eligibility

The dog park is open from 6 am to 10 pm. A dog using the park must be currently licensed and vaccinated per the requirements the Owner would need to show a veterinarian, and the handler should be prepared to provide proof if asked by management following a complaint. Only one dog per adult handler is permitted in the park at a time, regardless of how many pets that household keeps.

### 3.2 Dog park etiquette

Handlers must pick up and dispose of waste immediately, matching the Common Area pet rule in the Rules and Regulations. The dog park is not reservable and is shared on a first-come basis; a handler whose dog is aggressive toward another dog or person should leave the park promptly and may be asked by management to use it only at off-peak times going forward.

## Article 4 - Guest Suite

### 4.1 Purpose and eligibility

The guest suite is available for a resident's own visiting guests, not for the resident's personal use, independent short-term rental, or use by a non-resident third party unconnected to a Lot. A stay is limited to 5 nights per booking. Check-in is at 3 pm and check-out at 11 am. Using the guest suite as a workaround for the short-term rental ban in the Rules and Regulations, such as booking it repeatedly for an unrelated paying guest, is treated as a short-term rental violation under the Fine and Enforcement Policy.

### 4.2 Fees

The guest suite costs $60 per night plus a one-time $50 cleaning fee per stay, charged to the booking resident's assessment account at the time of booking or check-in. Fees unpaid after booking are treated as any other account charge under the Collection Policy, including the eventual loss-of-access consequence for a seriously delinquent account. A 5-night stay therefore costs $350 in total: $300 for the nights plus the $50 cleaning fee, which applies once per stay regardless of its length.

### 4.3 Booking procedure

A booking may be made up to 120 days in advance through the Summit resident app, which shows real-time availability since there is only one guest suite shared by all 96 Lots. A resident should book as early as possible for a popular date, such as a holiday weekend; for example, a Thanksgiving-week booking is often made months ahead given the limited inventory.

### 4.4 Cancellation and condition

A booking may be cancelled or shortened through the app; management asks for at least 48 hours' notice where possible so another resident can use the date. The booking resident is responsible for the condition of the suite during their guest's stay and for any damage beyond normal wear, billed to the resident's account if it exceeds the standard cleaning fee.

## Article 5 - General Amenity Rules

### 5.1 Household responsibility for guests

A resident is responsible for their guests' conduct at any amenity and may be billed or fined under the Fine and Enforcement Policy for a guest's violation exactly as if the resident had committed it themselves. This includes damage a guest causes to amenity equipment or the guest suite, which is billed to the resident's account alongside any applicable fine.

### 5.2 Suspension of access

Amenity access, including the pool, fitness room, dog park, and guest suite, may be suspended for an account more than 60 days delinquent, as described in the Collection Policy, or for up to 30 days following a pool or clubhouse rule violation under the Fine and Enforcement Policy. Access is restored automatically once management confirms the account is current or the suspension period has run, without a separate request from the Owner.
