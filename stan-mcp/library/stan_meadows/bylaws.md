# Bylaws of Stan Meadows Townhome Association

<!-- type: bylaws; adopted: 2011-06-01 -->

These Bylaws govern the internal affairs of Stan Meadows Townhome Association: its Board, officers, committees, and membership meetings. They implement the Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions and must be read together with it; where a conflict exists, the Declaration controls.

## Article 1 - Membership

### 1.1 Membership and voting weight

Every Owner of a Lot is a member of the Association for as long as they hold title. Each of the 96 Lots carries one vote. A member who is delinquent in assessments by more than 60 days may be denied the right to vote at a meeting until the account is brought current, except that this does not affect the Lot's right to notice.

### 1.2 Proxies

A member may vote by written proxy, signed and dated, naming another member or a specific person as proxy holder and delivered to the Secretary or management before the meeting begins. A proxy is valid for the meeting named in it, or for a period of up to 90 days if it so states, and may be revoked at any time before it is exercised.

## Article 2 - Meetings of the Membership

### 2.1 Annual meeting

The Association holds its annual membership meeting each February, at which the membership elects directors to fill expiring terms, receives the Board's and Treasurer's annual reports, and conducts other business properly brought before it. The annual meeting for the 2026-2027 board year is scheduled for February 2027, with one Board seat open, since Marcus Bell's term as a director ends in 2026. Nominations for the open seat may be submitted to management in advance or made from the floor at the meeting itself, and a candidate need only meet the good-standing qualification in Article 3.2 to be eligible.

### 2.2 Special meetings

A special meeting of the membership may be called by the President, by a majority of the Board, or by written petition of at least 25% of the Lots stating the purpose of the meeting. Notice of a special meeting must state its purpose, and no other business may be transacted at it. A petition calling a special meeting must be delivered to the Secretary or management, who will verify the signatures against the Lot ownership records before scheduling the meeting, normally within 30 days of a valid petition being received.

### 2.3 Notice

Written notice of any membership meeting, stating the date, time, place, and, for a special meeting, its purpose, must be given to every Owner at least 10 days and not more than 60 days before the meeting, by mail, email, or posting in the Summit resident app. Notice is considered given on the date it is mailed, emailed, or posted, not on the date an individual Owner happens to read it, and a single notice may be combined with the agenda circulated under the forum procedure in Section 2.5.

### 2.4 Quorum and voting

A quorum for a membership meeting is 25% of the Lots (24 homes), present in person or by proxy. If a quorum is not present, the meeting may be adjourned to a later date without further notice other than announcement at the meeting. Except where the Declaration requires a greater vote, such as the two-thirds vote to amend the Declaration, action is by majority of the votes present at a meeting where a quorum exists.

### 2.5 Owner forum and speaking time

Owners wishing to address the Board at a meeting should email the managing agent by noon the day before the meeting, identifying the topic. Each speaker is allowed three minutes during the open forum portion of the agenda, and the Board may limit repetitive comments on the same topic to keep the meeting on schedule.

## Article 3 - Board of Directors

### 3.1 Number and composition

The Board consists of not fewer than three and not more than five directors, each serving a two-year term, elected by the membership at the annual meeting so that terms are staggered. The Board currently has three directors: Helen Cho (President, term ending 2027), Marcus Bell (Treasurer, term ending 2026), and Ivy Nguyen (Secretary, term ending 2027).

### 3.2 Qualification and vacancy

A director must be an Owner in good standing, meaning no assessment more than 60 days delinquent and no unresolved architectural violation at the time of nomination. If a director resigns, is removed, or becomes unqualified mid-term, the remaining directors may appoint a replacement to serve until the next annual meeting, at which the seat is filled by election for the balance of the unexpired term if any remains.

### 3.3 Removal

A director may be removed before the end of their term by a majority vote of the membership at a special meeting called for that purpose, with notice to the director and an opportunity to be heard before the vote. A removal vote is tallied the same way as any other membership vote under Article 2, one vote per Lot, by ballot in person or by proxy, and the vacancy created is filled under the procedure in Section 3.2.

### 3.4 Powers and duties

The Board manages the business and affairs of the Association, including adopting the annual budget, levying assessments, enforcing the Governing Documents, contracting with vendors and the managing agent, and adopting policy resolutions such as the Rules and Regulations, the Fine and Enforcement Policy, the Collection Policy, and the Gate and Parking Policy. The Board may delegate day-to-day administration to the managing agent, currently Summit HOA Services (manager Brian Kowalski), without relieving itself of ultimate responsibility.

## Article 4 - Meetings of the Board

### 4.1 Regular meetings

The Board meets monthly, ordinarily on the second Tuesday, at a location and by the video option stated in the meeting notice. The next meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, November 10, 2026, at 6:30 pm, in the fitness room lounge and by video. Minutes of each meeting are posted in the Summit resident app. A regular meeting may be cancelled or moved by the President with reasonable notice to the other directors and to Owners, for example around a holiday, but the Board tries to keep a consistent monthly rhythm so Owners can plan to attend or speak.

### 4.2 Open meetings

Board meetings are open for Owners to observe, except when the Board goes into executive session under Section 4.4. An Owner wishing to speak follows the forum procedure in Section 2.5. An Owner who attends without pre-registering to speak may still be recognized during the open forum portion of the agenda at the President's discretion, time permitting, but pre-registered speakers are heard first.

### 4.3 Quorum and action

A majority of the directors then serving constitutes a quorum for Board business; with a three-member Board, two directors present satisfies quorum. Action is by majority vote of the directors present. A director may participate by phone or video and is counted present for quorum purposes, and a vote cast during a video session counts the same as one cast in person.

### 4.4 Executive session

The Board may meet in executive session, closed to Owners, to discuss legal matters, personnel, contract negotiations, or a specific Owner's delinquency or violation hearing. No vote that creates a financial obligation of the Association may be taken in executive session except final approval of a contract already discussed in open session; the vote itself must be announced at the next open meeting.

## Article 5 - Officers

### 5.1 Officers and election

The Board elects a President, Treasurer, and Secretary from among its members immediately after each annual meeting, each to serve a one-year term or until a successor is elected. The same director may not hold more than one officer position at a time. With a three-member Board, this means every director normally holds one of the three offices, so an officer election after the annual meeting ordinarily confirms the existing arrangement unless a new director has just been elected to replace one who is departing.

### 5.2 President

The President presides at Board and membership meetings, signs contracts and other documents on the Association's behalf as authorized by the Board, and is the primary point of contact between the Board and the managing agent on day-to-day matters. The President also casts the deciding voice on scheduling matters between meetings, such as confirming a special meeting date under Article 2.2, though any matter involving Association funds still requires a Board vote.

### 5.3 Treasurer

The Treasurer oversees the Association's finances, presents a financial report at each regular Board meeting, and works with the managing agent to prepare the annual budget for Board adoption, normally in December for the following fiscal year, and the periodic reserve study. The Treasurer also reviews the reserve fund balance at each meeting against the funding schedule in the current reserve study and flags to the Board any trend that might call for an earlier-than-planned update to the study or the reserve contribution rate.

### 5.4 Secretary

The Secretary keeps the minutes of Board and membership meetings, maintains the Association's official records, and sends or oversees the mailing of required notices to Owners. The Secretary is also responsible for certifying, on request, that a document such as a resolution or an amendment was properly adopted by the Board or the membership, which a title company or lender may ask for during a sale or refinance.

## Article 6 - Committees

### 6.1 Architectural Review Committee

The Architectural Review Committee reviews requests for exterior changes under Article 7 of the Declaration and the Architectural Guidelines. It meets monthly on the second Tuesday by video and reports its decisions to the Board. The Board appoints its members, who need not be directors, for one-year renewable terms. The Committee keeps a written record of every request and its decision, which the Board and management rely on when resolving a later dispute about what was actually approved for a given Lot.

### 6.2 Welcome Committee

The Welcome Committee greets new residents, providing a welcome binder with the Governing Documents and a set of gate fobs, and answers first questions about amenities and the resident app. It is advisory only and has no enforcement authority. Welcome Committee members are volunteers coordinated through management, and the Committee typically reaches out to a new household within the first two weeks after closing, based on the settlement information management receives from the title company.

### 6.3 Other committees

The Board may form other committees as needed, such as a social committee or a special ad hoc committee for a capital project, defining each committee's charge and, where relevant, its budget at the time of formation. An ad hoc committee dissolves automatically once it completes the task it was formed for, such as overseeing a single capital project, unless the Board votes to extend or convert it into a standing committee.

## Article 7 - Records and Inspection

### 7.1 Association records

The Association keeps its financial records, contracts, insurance policies, meeting minutes, and Owner account records, through the managing agent, for at least seven years. An Owner may inspect or request copies of records relating to the Association's finances and governance, other than records protected by attorney-client privilege or another Owner's personal account detail, on reasonable written request.

### 7.2 Minutes

Minutes of Board and membership meetings are posted in the Summit resident app within a reasonable time after approval and are available to any Owner on request before posting. Draft minutes are presented for approval at the following regular meeting under the standing agenda item for approval of prior minutes, and remain in draft form, subject to correction, until that approval occurs.

## Article 8 - Fiscal Matters

### 8.1 Fiscal year and budget

The Association's fiscal year is the calendar year. The Board adopts the budget for the coming year by December of the preceding year; the 2026 budget was adopted in December 2025. The budget sets the regular monthly assessment, currently $340 per Lot, due on the 1st of each month. If the Board has not adopted a new budget by January 1, the prior year's assessment continues unchanged until a new budget is adopted, so an Owner's monthly payment is never left undefined between fiscal years.

### 8.2 Financial reports

The Treasurer presents a report at each regular Board meeting comparing actual income and expense to budget, and an annual report to the membership at the annual meeting, including the status of the reserve fund. The monthly report is summarized in the minutes, and the full supporting financial statements prepared by the managing agent are available to any Owner on request under the records-inspection right in Section 7.1.

## Article 9 - Amendments to Bylaws

### 9.1 Amendment procedure

These Bylaws may be amended by a majority vote of the Lots at a membership meeting called with at least 10 days' notice stating the proposed amendment, or by two-thirds of the Board acting between membership meetings on a matter that does not change membership voting rights, subject to ratification at the next membership meeting. An amendment adopted by the Board under this provisional procedure is noted in the minutes as pending ratification and takes effect immediately, but lapses if the membership does not ratify it at the next meeting.
