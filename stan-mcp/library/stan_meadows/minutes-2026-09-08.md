# Minutes of the Board of Directors, Stan Meadows Townhome Association - September 8, 2026

<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-09-08 -->

These are the minutes of the regular monthly meeting of the Board of Directors of Stan Meadows Townhome Association, held Tuesday, September 8, 2026, at 6:30 pm in the fitness room lounge and by video, with management from Summit HOA Services in attendance.

## Business

### 1. Call to order and roll call

President Helen Cho called the meeting to order at 6:30 pm in the fitness room lounge, with the video line open for remote attendees. Treasurer Marcus Bell and Secretary Ivy Nguyen were also present, constituting a full Board quorum, along with property manager Brian Kowalski. Five Owners attended by video and one in person. Helen Cho noted two items added to the agenda since it was first circulated: the formal gate camera quote and the Peak Roofing siding proposal.

### 2. Approval of August minutes

Marcus Bell moved to approve the minutes of the August 11, 2026 regular meeting as circulated in the resident app. Ivy Nguyen seconded. The motion carried 3-0. No corrections were requested, and Ivy Nguyen confirmed the approved minutes would be posted to the resident app by the end of the week, alongside the September agenda once it was finalized.

### 3. Manager's report

Brian Kowalski reported that the pool season would close as scheduled on September 30 and that fob access to the pool deck would be deactivated October 1. He also reported that the two community-wide garage sale weekends for the year, including the September weekend, had gone smoothly with no reported parking complaints, and that the Welcome Committee had onboarded three new households in August, each receiving a binder and gate fobs.

### 4. Treasurer's report

Marcus Bell reported the Association remained on budget through August, with operating expenses tracking close to plan against the $391,680 annual budget. He reported the reserve fund at approximately $601,000 and reminded the Board that the 2025 reserve study projects roof replacement on Buildings 1 through 8 for 2031, which the current reserve contribution is designed to fund without a special assessment.

### 5. Architectural Review Committee report

The Committee reported three requests reviewed at its September meeting: one solar panel placement noted and approved, one landscaping request for drought-tolerant plantings approved on the fast-track review, and one privacy screen request near a patio denied as submitted because the proposed height exceeded the Architectural Guidelines, with the Owner invited to resubmit at a compliant height.

### 6. Gate camera upgrade

Brian Kowalski presented a formal quote from SecureEntry Systems of $18,000 for a full camera and recorder upgrade at the main gate, funded from the reserve fund. Marcus Bell moved to place the gate camera upgrade on the November 10, 2026 agenda for a final vote, allowing time for the quote to be posted to Owners in advance as required for a reserve-funded capital item of this size. Ivy Nguyen seconded. The motion carried 3-0.

### 7. Building 7 siding repair

Management presented a proposal from Peak Roofing to replace the warped siding panels identified in August, with scaffolding planned for the week of October 19 through 30, 2026, funded from the roof and siding reserve. Helen Cho moved to approve the Peak Roofing proposal and schedule. Marcus Bell seconded. The motion carried 3-0. Management was asked to notify Building 7 residents of the scaffolding dates and any related gate or parking adjustments.

### 8. Dog park turf

Management reported a turf replacement quote of approximately $3,200, to be funded from reserves. The Board asked management to confirm scheduling with the vendor for early spring 2027, after the ground thaws, and to bring a final cost to the November meeting alongside the gate camera vote. Marcus Bell noted the figure was in line with what the dog park maintenance line in the budget could reasonably absorb without a separate reserve draw, pending the final number.

### 9. Homeowner forum

A homeowner from Unit 5B asked whether the fitness room hours could be extended earlier than 5 am; Helen Cho noted the current hours were set with vendor and insurance considerations in mind and asked management to review the history before any change is considered. A homeowner from Unit 12A thanked the Board for the fast turnaround on a recent drought-tolerant landscaping approval.

### 10. Adjournment

There being no further business, Helen Cho adjourned the meeting at 7:34 pm. The next regular meeting was set for Tuesday, November 10, 2026, at 6:30 pm, with the gate camera upgrade and the dog park turf cost scheduled for a final vote. Brian Kowalski agreed to post the November agenda to the resident app at least ten days ahead, consistent with the Bylaws' notice requirement, so Owners wishing to speak on either item could plan to attend.
