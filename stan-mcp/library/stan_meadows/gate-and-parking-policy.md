# Gate and Parking Policy of Stan Meadows Townhome Association

<!-- type: policy; adopted: 2012-02-01; amended: 2019-04-15, 2026-07-01 -->

Stan Meadows is a fully gated community, and this policy sets out how residents, guests, vendors, and contractors get through the gate and where they may park once inside. It implements the gate-related provisions of the Declaration and the Rules and Regulations and is the document referenced in the Governing Documents list as the "gate policy."

## Article 1 - Gate Credentials

### 1.1 Personal gate codes

Every household receives a personal gate code, issued by management and entered at the vehicle gate keypad or used in the Summit resident app. Codes change every January 1 for all households at once, and the new code is sent to each Owner's address and email on file before the change takes effect. A resident should never give their personal code to a guest, contractor, or delivery service; those parties use the credentials described in Articles 2 and 3 instead.

### 1.2 Gate fobs

Each household also receives a set of gate fobs, provided at move-in as part of the Welcome Committee's binder, for pedestrian and secondary access points. A lost fob should be reported to management immediately so it can be deactivated; a replacement fob costs $25, charged to the Owner's account. Reporting a lost fob promptly matters because, unlike a gate code, a fob is not changed community-wide each year, so an unreported lost fob stays active until someone notices it missing.

## Article 2 - Guest Access

### 2.1 Guest passes

A resident expecting a guest should issue that guest a one-time or limited-use pass through the Summit resident app rather than sharing a personal gate code. A guest pass can be set to expire after a single entry or after a chosen number of days, and the app logs which household issued each pass for security purposes.

### 2.2 Guest suite visitors

A guest staying in the Association's guest suite, described in the Amenities and Guest Suite Policy, receives a pass covering the length of their stay, issued automatically when the booking resident completes check-in. The pass expires at the suite's 11 am check-out time on the last booked day, and the booking resident can extend it through the app if the stay is lengthened and the suite remains available.

## Article 3 - Vendor, Contractor and Delivery Access

### 3.1 Scheduled vendor access

The Association's regular vendors, including Evergreen Grounds, SecureEntry Systems, Crystal Pools, and Peak Roofing, hold standing gate credentials for their scheduled service days. An Owner's personal contractor, such as someone doing interior work or a one-time repair, is not automatically on this list and needs a guest-style pass arranged by the Owner or by calling management during office hours.

### 3.2 Project access during major work

During a scheduled capital project, such as the Building 7 siding repair with scaffolding planned for October 19 through 30, 2026, management arranges standing access for the contractor's crew and vehicles for the duration of the project and notifies nearby Owners in advance of the gate traffic and any temporary parking restrictions near the work area.

### 3.3 Delivery services

Routine package and food delivery services typically use a call box or a standing arrangement with the gate vendor and do not require an individual resident to meet them at the gate, but a resident expecting an unusual or oversized delivery should treat the driver as a guest under Article 2.

## Article 4 - Parking

### 4.1 Resident parking

Each townhome's two-car garage is the resident's assigned parking, and residents must use it for their own household vehicles rather than leaving them in a marked visitor space overnight. A household with more vehicles than garage space should contact management to discuss options rather than relying on visitor spaces as regular overflow parking, since those spaces exist for guests and are monitored for exactly that purpose under Article 5.

### 4.2 Visitor spaces

Marked visitor spaces are for guests holding a valid pass under Article 2, for up to three consecutive nights. A visitor space is not assigned to any one Lot and is available on a first-come basis within the posted limit. A guest needing to stay longer than three nights should instead book the Association's guest suite under the Amenities and Guest Suite Policy, or the resident should ask management about a documented exception for an unusual circumstance.

## Article 5 - Enforcement and Towing

### 5.1 Unauthorized vehicles

A vehicle parked in a visitor space without a valid guest pass, or a resident vehicle left in a visitor space overnight in violation of Section 4.1, may be tagged with a warning notice. If it remains in violation after the warning period stated on the tag, typically 24 hours, it may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense, in addition to any fine the Owner incurs under the Fine and Enforcement Policy for a repeated violation.

### 5.2 Fire lanes and blocked access

A vehicle blocking a fire lane, a garage, mailbox cluster access, or the main gate itself may be towed immediately without a warning period, given the safety and access concerns involved. Management or any director may authorize an immediate tow in this situation without waiting for a regular Board meeting, and will document the circumstances in the Association's records afterward.

## Article 6 - Gate Malfunctions and Emergencies

### 6.1 Reporting a stuck or malfunctioning gate

A gate that will not open or close should be reported to the after-hours line at 555-0157, which reaches management or its emergency contractor outside office hours (weekdays 8:30 am to 4:30 pm); during office hours, call the main management line at 555-0156. Management arranges for SecureEntry Systems to respond and may temporarily leave the gate in a safe open or manual-release position if needed for resident access until repaired.

### 6.2 Emergency vehicle access

Police, fire, and emergency medical vehicles have an independent override to open the gate immediately and do not require a resident or guest pass. A resident with a life-threatening emergency should call 911 first; emergency responders are equipped to enter Stan Meadows without delay. SecureEntry Systems maintains this override as part of its service contract, independent of the household code cycle and the resident app, so it keeps working even during a broader gate malfunction.
