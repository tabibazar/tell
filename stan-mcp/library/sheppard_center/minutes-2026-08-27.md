# Minutes of the Sheppard Center HOA Board of Directors, August 27, 2026
<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-08-27 -->

Regular monthly meeting of the Board of Directors of the Sheppard Center Homeowners Association, held Thursday, August 27, 2026, at 7 pm in the clubhouse at 1 Meadow Circle, and by video. Directors present: Margaret Hale (President), Tom Reyes (Vice President), Anita Brooks (Treasurer), Kevin O'Neill (Secretary) and Lena Park (Member at Large). Also present: Dana Ortiz, community manager, Lakeside Property Management.

## Business

### 1. Call to order and roll call

President Margaret Hale called the meeting to order at 7:02 pm in the clubhouse at 1 Meadow Circle. Secretary Kevin O'Neill called the roll and confirmed all five directors were present in person, establishing a quorum under Article 7 of the Bylaws. Ms. Hale welcomed the homeowners present in the clubhouse and those joining by video, and reviewed the evening's agenda, noting that the clubhouse roof and the October special assessment proposal would be the main items of business.

### 2. Approval of previous minutes

Secretary Kevin O'Neill presented the draft minutes of the July 23, 2026 regular meeting for the Board's review. Mr. Reyes moved to approve the minutes as presented; Ms. Brooks seconded the motion. There was no discussion. Motion carried 5-0, with Hale, Reyes, Brooks, O'Neill and Park all voting aye, and the approved minutes were directed to be posted to the owner portal within the usual 30-day window.

### 3. Treasurer's report

Treasurer Anita Brooks reported the Association's reserve fund stood at approximately $409,000, tracking toward the reserve study's projected year-end figure, with operating accounts current and no unusual variances against the 2026 budget through July. Ms. Brooks noted that the clubhouse roof, flagged in the 2024 Northgate Reserve Consultants study as a 2026 priority, had been inspected again in August and continues to show accelerating wear that will likely require replacement before winter.

### 4. Clubhouse roof and pool resurfacing

Ms. Brooks reported that a contractor's inspection estimated the clubhouse roof project at roughly $130,000, more than the reserve fund can absorb without jeopardizing the pool resurfacing project targeted for 2027. She recommended the Board consider a special assessment to cover part of the roof cost, to be presented for a vote at the October meeting. Mr. Reyes moved to direct management to prepare a special assessment proposal of $400 per home for the October 22 agenda, with the balance of the roof cost funded from reserves; Ms. Park seconded. Motion carried 5-0. Separately, the Board directed management to solicit at least three bids for the 2027 pool resurfacing project so figures are ready well before that budget year.

### 5. Architectural Review Committee report

Committee Chair Paul Wexler's written report, read by Ms. Hale in his absence, noted the committee's August 3 meeting reviewed three applications, including a fence replacement request from the Owner of Lot 22 to replace an aging wood fence with white vinyl. The committee was still discussing the gate's proposed orientation and expected to finalize its decision at the September meeting. The report also noted two uncontested repaint applications using existing approved colors, both approved administratively without needing to wait for the monthly meeting, and a reminder to Owners that any exterior repaint in a new color, not just a new structure, still requires an application before the paint goes on.

### 6. Violation update

Ms. Ortiz reported on an open parking violation involving a trailer parked on the street in front of a rented home on Lot 187 since August 14, in excess of the 48-hour loading limit for trailers. A courtesy notice had been sent to the Owner, who had properly registered the lease with management, giving 14 days to cure the condition, which placed the cure deadline at August 28; the Owner had responded promptly that the tenant was arranging to move the trailer before then. Management was continuing to monitor the Lot for compliance before recommending any further step to the Board.

### 7. Social committee and upcoming events

Social Committee Chair Rosa Diaz reported that planning for the fall festival on Saturday, October 17, from 1 to 5 pm on the clubhouse lawn was underway, with food trucks and a bouncy castle already booked, and asked for volunteers to help with setup. The Board also noted the annual shredding truck event would return to the clubhouse parking lot on October 24.

### 8. Homeowner open forum

Two homeowners signed up to speak. The Owner of Lot 61 asked about the process for requesting approval of a future rooftop solar installation; Ms. Ortiz explained that an architectural application would go to the committee, which cannot deny a solar request on appearance alone, and will respond within 30 days. The Owner of Lot 114 asked about plans to address algae buildup near the Heron Pond overlook; Landscape Committee Chair Greg Lund, present in the audience, said GreenEdge Landscapes would treat the area in September.

### 9. Adjournment

With no further business before the Board, Ms. Park moved to adjourn the meeting; Mr. O'Neill seconded the motion. Motion carried 5-0. The meeting adjourned at 8:14 pm. The next regular meeting of the Board was set for Thursday, September 24, 2026, at 7 pm in the clubhouse and by video, with the special assessment proposal and the draft 2027 budget expected to be discussed further before the October vote.
