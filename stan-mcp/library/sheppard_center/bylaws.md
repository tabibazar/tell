# Bylaws of the Sheppard Center Homeowners Association
<!-- type: bylaws; adopted: 1998-06-01; amended: 2015-04-12, 2019-11-21 -->

These Bylaws govern the internal affairs of the Sheppard Center Homeowners Association: membership and voting, the Board of Directors, officers, committees, meetings and records. They supplement the Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions and must be read together with it; where the two conflict, the Declaration controls.

## Article 1 - Name and Purpose

### 1.1 Name and Purpose

The name of the Association is the Sheppard Center Homeowners Association. Its purpose is to own, operate and maintain the Common Area of Sheppard Center, a community of 212 single-family homes in Maple Grove, to enforce the Declaration, the Rules and Regulations and the Architectural Guidelines, and to manage the Association's finances for the benefit of its members. Membership in the Association is automatic and mandatory for every Owner of a Lot and ends automatically when ownership of the Lot transfers.

## Article 2 - Membership and Voting

### 2.1 Voting Rights

Each Lot carries one vote, regardless of the number of persons who own it. Where a Lot has more than one Owner, the Owners must agree among themselves how that single vote is cast; the Association is not responsible for resolving a disagreement between co-owners over a Lot's vote. A vote may be cast by an Owner present at a meeting, by proxy, or electronically where the Board has authorized electronic voting for that matter.

### 2.2 Proxies and Electronic Voting

An Owner unable to attend a meeting may vote by proxy using the form provided by the management company, Lakeside Property Management, which must be signed and delivered to management before the meeting. For matters the Board designates for electronic voting, such as the annual budget or a special assessment, the electronic voting window opens 14 days before the meeting at which the matter will be decided and closes at the start of that meeting.

## Article 3 - Board of Directors

### 3.1 Composition and Terms

The Association is governed by a Board of five directors elected by the membership, serving staggered terms so that not all seats are up for election in the same year. The current Board consists of Margaret Hale (President, term ending 2027), Tom Reyes (Vice President, term ending 2026), Anita Brooks (Treasurer, term ending 2027), Kevin O'Neill (Secretary, term ending 2026) and Lena Park (Member at Large, term ending 2028). A director must be an Owner in good standing for the duration of their term.

### 3.2 Powers and Duties

The Board manages the business and affairs of the Association, including adopting the annual budget, setting the regular and any special assessment, hiring and overseeing the management company, enforcing the governing documents, and appointing committees. The Board acts by majority vote of the directors present at a properly noticed meeting at which a quorum is present.

## Article 4 - Officers

### 4.1 Officers and Duties

The officers are the President, Vice President, Treasurer and Secretary, each elected by the Board from among its directors following the annual meeting. The President presides at meetings and is the Association's principal representative; the Vice President performs the President's duties in their absence; the Treasurer oversees the Association's finances, bank accounts and the annual budget presented to the Board, currently Anita Brooks; and the Secretary keeps the minutes of Board and membership meetings and the Association's official records, currently Kevin O'Neill.

## Article 5 - Elections

### 5.1 Annual Election

Directors are elected at the annual meeting of the membership, held each January; the next annual meeting is scheduled for January 21, 2027, with two Board seats up for election corresponding to the terms of Tom Reyes and Kevin O'Neill, both ending in 2026. A homeowner wishing to run for the Board must file as a candidate with management no later than December 10 of the year preceding the annual meeting.

### 5.2 Nomination and Ballot Procedure

Management compiles the list of candidates who filed by the deadline and distributes it to all Owners with the annual meeting notice, together with a proxy and ballot form. Write-in candidates are not permitted after the candidate list has been distributed. Votes are counted by management or by volunteers it appoints, under the observation of at least one director who is not a candidate, and results are announced before the annual meeting adjourns.

## Article 6 - Meetings

### 6.1 Board Meetings

The Board holds a regular meeting on the fourth Thursday of each month at 7 pm, in the clubhouse at 1 Meadow Circle and also by video for Owners who cannot attend in person. Notice of the regular meeting, including its agenda, is posted and distributed by management at least several days in advance. The Board may also call a special meeting on shorter notice when circumstances require.

### 6.2 Annual and Special Membership Meetings

The annual membership meeting is held each January and includes the Board election described in Article 5. A special membership meeting may be called by the Board or by petition of Owners representing at least 20% of the Lots, on a matter requiring a membership vote, such as an amendment to the Declaration or a special assessment that exceeds the Board's authority to levy on its own.

### 6.3 Homeowner Forum and Speaking Rules

Every regular Board meeting includes a homeowner open forum. An Owner wishing to speak must sign up with management at least 24 hours before the meeting and is limited to three minutes. The Board may, at its discretion, respond briefly to a forum comment but is not required to take action on it at that meeting; a matter requiring Board action is typically placed on a future agenda.

## Article 7 - Quorum

### 7.1 Quorum Requirements

A quorum for the annual membership meeting is 20% of the Lots, which is 43 of the 212 homes, present in person, by proxy or by electronic vote. A quorum for a Board meeting is a majority of the directors then serving, which is three of the five seats. No business requiring a vote may be conducted at a meeting without the applicable quorum, except to adjourn to a later date.

## Article 8 - Committees

### 8.1 Standing Committees

The Board appoints standing committees to advise it and to carry out specific functions. The Architectural Review Committee, chaired by Paul Wexler, meets the first Monday of each month at 6:30 pm in the clubhouse to review applications under the Architectural Guidelines. The Social Committee, chaired by Rosa Diaz, plans community events including the fall festival, the pool party and the holiday lights contest. The Landscape Committee, chaired by Greg Lund, advises the Board on common-area planting and the health of Heron Pond. The Neighbourhood Watch is a volunteer group that organizes a monthly walk and can be reached through management; it is not a security patrol and the Association does not contract for one.

## Article 9 - Records

### 9.1 Minutes and Records

The Secretary, with management's assistance, keeps minutes of every Board and membership meeting. Once approved, minutes are posted to the Lakeside owner portal within 30 days. The Association retains Board minutes for at least the preceding three years and makes its financial records, the governing documents and committee records available to Owners for inspection on reasonable request to management.

## Article 10 - Indemnification

### 10.1 Director and Officer Indemnification

The Association indemnifies its directors and officers against claims arising from the good-faith performance of their duties, to the fullest extent permitted by law, and maintains insurance covering that exposure as part of its master insurance program. This protection does not extend to willful misconduct or a knowing violation of law. A director or officer seeking indemnification must cooperate with the Association and its insurer in defending the claim, and the Board may require an accounting of any settlement or judgment before final reimbursement is approved.

## Article 11 - Vacancies and Removal

### 11.1 Filling a Vacancy

If a director resigns, is removed, or is otherwise unable to complete a term, the remaining directors may appoint a replacement to serve the balance of the unexpired term, provided the appointee is an Owner in good standing. A vacancy filled this way does not change the regular election schedule for that seat; the seat still comes up for election at the next annual meeting at which its term would otherwise have ended.

### 11.2 Removal of a Director

A director may be removed before the end of their term by a vote of Owners representing a majority of the Lots at a special membership meeting called for that purpose, with the proposed removal stated in the meeting notice so the director and the membership have fair notice of the matter. A director subject to a removal vote may address the membership before the vote is taken.

## Article 12 - Fiscal Year and Audit

### 12.1 Fiscal Year and Financial Review

The Association's fiscal year runs from January 1 through December 31, matching the calendar year used in its annual budget. The Treasurer presents a financial report at each regular Board meeting and an annual summary to the membership at the annual meeting. The Board arranges an independent review or audit of the Association's finances periodically, as it determines is prudent, and the results are made available to Owners on request to management.

## Article 13 - Amendments

### 13.1 Amending These Bylaws

These Bylaws may be amended by a majority vote of Owners at a meeting called for that purpose, or by the electronic or proxy voting procedure described in Article 2, with notice of the proposed amendment given to all Owners at least 14 days in advance. These Bylaws were adopted in 1998 and have since been amended to update the proxy procedure in 2015 and to add electronic voting in 2019.
