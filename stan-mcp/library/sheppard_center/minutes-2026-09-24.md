# Minutes of the Sheppard Center HOA Board of Directors, September 24, 2026
<!-- type: minutes; adopted: 2026-09-24 -->

Regular monthly meeting of the Board of Directors of the Sheppard Center Homeowners Association, held Thursday, September 24, 2026, at 7 pm in the clubhouse at 1 Meadow Circle, and by video. Directors present: Margaret Hale (President), Anita Brooks (Treasurer), Kevin O'Neill (Secretary) and Lena Park (Member at Large). Tom Reyes (Vice President) was excused. Also present: Dana Ortiz, community manager, Lakeside Property Management.

## Business

### 1. Call to order and roll call

President Margaret Hale called the meeting to order at 7 pm in the clubhouse at 1 Meadow Circle. Secretary Kevin O'Neill called the roll and noted four of five directors present, a quorum under Article 7 of the Bylaws, with Vice President Tom Reyes excused for a family commitment. Ms. Hale outlined the evening's agenda, noting that placing the dues increase and special assessment on the October ballot was the night's priority item.

### 2. Approval of previous minutes

Mr. O'Neill presented the draft minutes of the August 27, 2026 regular meeting for the Board's review. Ms. Park moved to approve them as presented; Ms. Brooks seconded the motion. There was no discussion or correction requested. Motion carried 4-0, with Hale, Brooks, O'Neill and Park all voting aye, and the minutes were directed to be posted to the owner portal in the usual manner.

### 3. Treasurer's report

Treasurer Anita Brooks reported the reserve fund balance at approximately $412,000, consistent with the 2024 Northgate Reserve Consultants study's estimate of about 61% funded. She presented a draft 2027 budget recommending the regular monthly assessment rise from $285.00 to $299.00 to keep pace with landscaping, insurance and management costs, and confirmed the clubhouse roof special assessment of $400 per home discussed in August was ready to be presented formally in October. Ms. Brooks also reported that bids for the 2027 pool resurfacing project had come in from two vendors so far, with a third expected before the October meeting.

### 4. October 22 agenda and voting window

Mr. O'Neill moved to place both the proposed 2027 dues increase to $299.00 per month and the $400 per home clubhouse roof special assessment, due January 15, 2027 if approved, on the October 22 meeting agenda for a membership vote, with electronic voting to open October 8, fourteen days before the meeting as the Bylaws require. Ms. Brooks seconded. Motion carried 4-0. Ms. Ortiz confirmed management would distribute voting materials to all 212 homes by October 1.

### 5. Architectural Review Committee report

Reporting for the committee, Ms. Hale noted that at its September 7 meeting the Architectural Review Committee approved the fence replacement application for the Owner of Lot 22, permitting a white vinyl fence up to 6 feet with the condition that the gate face the side yard rather than the front; the written approval was issued to the Owner on September 9 once the condition was documented. The same meeting flagged a shed at Lot 114 that had been repainted in a color not on the approved palette, which management followed up on with a site visit and a courtesy notice, discussed further under violations below.

### 6. Violation updates

Ms. Ortiz reported that the parking violation involving a trailer at Lot 187 discussed in August was resolved on September 2 with no fine, after the tenant removed the trailer from the street within days of the courtesy notice's cure deadline; because the Owner had acted promptly and before any further notice was issued, the Board did not pursue a fine. She also reported that the shed-color matter at Lot 114 flagged under item 5 resulted in a courtesy notice issued September 20 for a shed painted in a color not on the approved palette, with a 14-day cure period running to October 4. Given the Owner's dispute over whether the color qualifies as the approved Sage, raised again in the open forum under item 9, the Board directed management to place the matter on the October 22 agenda as a hearing in case it remains uncured or unresolved by the October 4 deadline.

### 7. Social committee and community events

Social Committee Chair Rosa Diaz confirmed the fall festival for Saturday, October 17, from 1 to 5 pm on the clubhouse lawn is fully booked with vendors, and reminded the Board that the shredding truck will be in the clubhouse parking lot on October 24 from 9 am to noon, with a limit of three boxes per household. Ms. Diaz also said early planning had begun for the December 18 holiday lights contest judging.

### 8. Annual meeting reminder

Mr. O'Neill reminded the Board that the annual membership meeting is set for January 21, 2027, with two Board seats up for election, currently held by Mr. Reyes and Mr. O'Neill, both of whose terms end in 2026. Candidates must file as candidates with management no later than December 10, 2026, after which the candidate list is finalized and no write-in candidates are accepted. The Board asked management to include a reminder of the filing deadline and a short description of the role in the October homeowner mailing, so interested Owners have time to decide before the deadline.

### 9. Homeowner open forum

Three homeowners signed up to speak. The Owner of Lot 114 addressed the Board about the courtesy notice discussed under item 6, stating that the color applied is, in their view, close to the approved "Sage" body color and asking the Board to consider approving it as a match rather than requiring a repaint before the October 4 cure deadline; Ms. Hale noted the question would be taken up at the October 22 hearing if not resolved before then, where the Owner could bring a paint sample for comparison against the approved palette. The Owner of Lot 61 asked about timing for a planned rooftop solar application; Ms. Ortiz confirmed the committee would review it at its November 2 meeting once filed. A resident of Heron Court asked whether the Association could add a bench along the walking loop; Mr. Lund said the landscape committee would look into cost and placement and report back at a future meeting, possibly alongside the pond's algae treatment update already underway with GreenEdge Landscapes.

### 10. Adjournment

With no further business before the Board, Ms. Brooks moved to adjourn the meeting; Ms. Park seconded the motion. Motion carried 4-0. The meeting adjourned at 8:21 pm. The next regular meeting of the Board was set for Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 7 pm in the clubhouse and by video, when the dues increase, the special assessment and, if still unresolved after October 4, the Lot 114 shed-color hearing are all scheduled to be taken up.
