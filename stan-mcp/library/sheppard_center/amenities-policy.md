# Amenities Policy for Sheppard Center
<!-- type: amenities; adopted: 2005-03-01; amended: 2022-05-10 -->

Sheppard Center's Common Area includes a clubhouse, a pool, two tennis courts, a playground and the 1.2-mile walking loop around Heron Pond. This Amenities Policy sets the hours, access rules, fees and reservation procedures for each of these amenities, in addition to the general Common Area provisions in Article 8 of the Declaration.

## Part 1 - Pool

### 1.1 Pool Hours and Access

The pool is open seasonally from Memorial Day through Labor Day, daily from 8 am to 9 pm, with a dedicated lap swim period from 6 am to 8 am on weekdays before general hours begin. Access requires a pool key fob issued by management; a lost or additional fob costs $25.00 to replace. The pool is closed for the season outside these months; it closed for 2026 on September 8 and will reopen in May 2027.

### 1.2 Pool Rules

Each home may bring up to two guests to the pool at a time. Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult. Glass containers of any kind are not allowed in the pool area. A lifeguard is on duty on weekends only; swimming outside lifeguard hours is at each swimmer's own risk. Smoking is not permitted in the pool area, and violations of pool rules, including bringing glass or exceeding the guest limit, carry the $100 fine described in the Fine and Enforcement Policy and may result in a 30-day suspension of pool access.

## Part 2 - Clubhouse

### 2.1 Clubhouse Hours and Rental

The clubhouse at 1 Meadow Circle is open daily from 8 am to 11 pm, and any rented event must end by 11 pm. Capacity is limited to 80 people. An Owner may rent the clubhouse for a private event for a $75.00 rental fee plus a $300.00 refundable damage deposit, returned after the event if the space is left in good condition. Smoking is not permitted inside the clubhouse at any time, consistent with the Rules and Regulations.

### 2.2 Booking the Clubhouse

A clubhouse rental may be booked up to 90 days in advance through management, for a single booking of up to 6 hours. To keep access fair across 212 homes, each home is limited to one weekend clubhouse booking per month. Management's office is open weekdays 9 am to 5 pm and also at the clubhouse office on Tuesdays from 4 pm to 7 pm, which is a convenient time to book a reservation in person.

## Part 3 - Tennis Courts

### 3.1 Tennis Court Hours and Reservations

The two tennis courts are open from 7 am to 10 pm, with lights available until 10 pm for evening play. There is no fee to use the courts. A court may be reserved up to 7 days in advance through management. When other players are waiting for a court, play is limited to 90-minute slots so everyone gets a turn, even without a formal reservation.

## Part 4 - Walking Loop

### 4.1 Walking Loop Access

The 1.2-mile walking loop around Heron Pond is open from dawn to dusk and requires no reservation or fee. Dogs must remain leashed on the loop at all times, and motorized bikes, including electric scooters and similar devices, are not permitted; the loop is reserved for walking, jogging and non-motorized cycling. As described in the Rules and Regulations, swimming, boating, fishing and ice skating in Heron Pond itself are never permitted, regardless of the season.

## Part 5 - Playground

### 5.1 Playground Access and Rules

The playground is open from dawn to dusk at no charge and requires no reservation. It is intended for children between the ages of 2 and 12, who must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising play and for any injury resulting from a lack of supervision, since the Association does not staff the playground.

## Part 6 - General Amenity Provisions

### 6.1 Standing and Suspension of Access

An Owner's account must be in good standing to use the reservable amenities; an account more than 90 days delinquent, or an Owner under an active 30-day amenity suspension from a sustained pool or clubhouse violation, may have their pool fob or clubhouse booking privileges temporarily deactivated by management until the balance is resolved or the suspension period ends. Household members and registered tenants of an Owner in good standing retain normal access even if that specific suspension is tied to a different household member's conduct, unless the Board directs otherwise in a particular case. A registered tenant under a long-term lease is treated as the household in good standing for amenity purposes, using the Lot's fob and booking privileges in place of the Owner for the duration of the tenancy.

## Part 7 - Guest Policies and Amenity Damage

### 7.1 Guests at the Amenities

The two-guest limit at the pool described in Part 1 applies per home per visit, not per household member, so a family of four may still bring only two guests at a time regardless of how many residents are present. At the clubhouse, a renting Owner's guest count is governed by the 80-person capacity limit rather than a per-home guest count, since the clubhouse rental is for a single event rather than everyday amenity use. Guests at the tennis courts, walking loop and playground are welcome without a numeric limit, consistent with the informal, non-reservable nature of those spaces, though a resident host remains responsible for a guest's conduct in all cases.

### 7.2 Seasonal and Weather Closures

Any amenity may be closed temporarily by management for weather, maintenance or safety reasons, such as closing the pool during a lightning warning or closing the walking loop's paths after an ice storm until the Association's snow and ice removal crew can clear them. A seasonal closure, such as the pool's close for the year after Labor Day, follows the fixed calendar described in Part 1 rather than a day-to-day decision, while a weather or maintenance closure is announced as it happens, typically through the owner portal and a posted sign at the amenity itself. No refund or credit is owed for a closure due to weather, safety or routine maintenance, since amenity access is a shared benefit of ownership rather than a paid service with a guaranteed schedule.

### 7.3 Damage to Amenities

An Owner is responsible for damage their household or guests cause to Common Area amenities, including the clubhouse, pool area furniture, tennis court fencing or playground equipment, beyond ordinary wear. For a rented clubhouse event, the $300.00 refundable deposit described in Part 2 is applied first to any repair cost; if the damage exceeds the deposit, the Owner is billed for the difference, added to their account and collectible like any other assessment under the Collection Policy. Damage discovered after a non-rental use of an amenity, such as the pool or tennis courts, is investigated by management, which may rely on fob or booking records to identify the responsible household.
